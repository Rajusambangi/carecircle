# CareCircle — Voice-over script (≈ 4 min 15 s)

**How to use:** read the normal text aloud. Lines in *[brackets]* are what to do on screen — don't read them.
Speak slowly (about 140 words a minute). Where you see **(pause)**, stop for a second and let the screen catch up. Answers take a few seconds to appear; keep talking over the animation, or pause and cut the wait later.

---

## 1. Introduction — 0:00 to 0:30
*[Screen: CareCircle open on the Ask tab, welcome screen visible.]*

Hi, I'm Raju, and this is CareCircle.

A friend's father fell last month. Four people had seen the warning signs — the nurse, his son, his daughter — but none of them knew the others had.

CareCircle is one shared memory for everyone caring for an elderly parent, built on Hindsight agent memory. Everyone logs what they notice, and the agent connects the dots. **(pause)**

## 2. Meet the patient — 0:30 to 0:55
*[Click the "Ramesh Sharma" card at the top right. The profile opens.]*

This is Ramesh, seventy-four, living alone in Pune, with diabetes, high blood pressure and knee arthritis.

His care circle is his daughter Priya, his son Arjun in Seattle, and Lakshmi, his nurse. He sees two doctors.

His medicines here come straight from memory, and this is how many memories the family has built together. **(pause)**

*[Close the profile.]*

## 3. Memory versus no memory — 0:55 to 1:40
*[On the Ask tab, make sure "vs no memory" is selected. Click the suggestion "Dad has been dizzy again this morning. What should I tell the cardiologist?"]*

Let's ask a real question: "Dad has been dizzy again this morning. What should I tell the cardiologist?"

On the left, the same AI with no memory. Sensible advice — for anyone. It doesn't know Ramesh. **(pause)**

*[Point to the right-hand card as it types out.]*

On the right is CareCircle. It knows the cardiologist doubled his Metoprolol on August tenth, and the dizziness started just three days later. It was reported by three different people — the nurse, his son and his daughter. There was a fall on August twenty-second. And the cholesterol test the cardiologist asked for back in July still hasn't been done.

*[Point to the green "All dates match" badge, then click "Based on … memories".]*

Every date is checked against the family's records, and every source shows who recorded it. **(pause)**

## 4. Watch it learn — 1:40 to 2:15
*[Click "New conversation", then click "Watch CareCircle learn".]*

Now the same question, asked of CareCircle's memory as it was on four different days.

*[Point along the progress bar, then at each column as it fills.]*

With no memory — generic. After one week — it knows his medicines, but nothing about dizziness yet. By day forty-five — it has just seen the dose change and the first dizzy spells. And today — it connects everything.

The AI model is the same in all four. The only thing that changed is what's in memory. **(pause)**

## 5. Logging by voice, and a live alert — 2:15 to 2:55
*[Click the Log tab. "I am" should be Priya.]*

This is the Log screen, where anyone records what happened — by typing, or speaking in English, Hindi or Marathi.

*[Click the mic and say clearly: "Dr. Mehta called with the urine culture result and wants to start Bactrim DS twice a day for five days." Stop the mic, then click "Save to memory".]*

Priya just got a call from the family doctor. **(pause)**

*[The red alert slides in.]*

And CareCircle flags it straight away. In March 2024, Bactrim gave Ramesh a rash, and the doctor said to avoid that type of antibiotic. Nobody remembered that today — but the memory did.

It doesn't tell Priya what to do — it tells her what to raise with the doctor.

*[Point to "What CareCircle just learned".]*

Down here you can see exactly what the memory learned from that one sentence. **(pause)**

## 6. Catch-up for the son abroad — 2:55 to 3:20
*[Change "I am" to Arjun. Click "What's new".]*

Now I'm Arjun, the son in Seattle. He last checked two weeks ago.

Instead of scrolling a family WhatsApp group, he gets only what changed, who reported it, and what he can do. **(pause)**

*[Close the panel. Switch "I am" back to Priya.]*

## 7. Doctor visit brief — 3:20 to 3:45
*[Click the Doctor brief tab. Choose Dr. Sunita Kulkarni, last visit August 10, upcoming visit September 30. Click "Generate brief".]*

On Wednesday, Ramesh sees his cardiologist. CareCircle writes her a one-page brief — medicine changes and what followed, trends with real numbers, the overdue test, and questions to ask. **(pause)**

## 8. Timeline and care profile — 3:45 to 4:10
*[Click the Timeline tab and scroll a little.]*

The Timeline is everything the family has recorded, month by month, and you can search it.

*[Click the Care profile tab.]*

And the care profile is what CareCircle has learned about Ramesh — his medicines, past reactions, and small things that matter, like explaining medicines in Marathi and booking afternoon appointments. It updates itself as the family logs. **(pause)**

## 9. Closing — 4:10 to 4:30
*[Go back to the Ask tab.]*

What surprised me: the clever part didn't come from prompt tricks. It came from what we store in memory — when things really happened, and who said them. Get that right, and Hindsight connects the dots.

CareCircle never diagnoses. It remembers, connects and tells the family what to take to the doctor.

The code is linked below. Thanks for watching — because your family forgets, but CareCircle doesn't.

### Shorter version (≈ 2 min 30 s)
Read sections **1, 3, 4, 5 and 9** only.

### Before recording
- Run every question once beforehand so answers appear quickly, then click **New conversation**.
- Browser zoom 110–125%, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off.
- Record at 1080p (QuickTime: **Cmd + Shift + 5**, choose your microphone in Options).
- If you stumble, pause two seconds and re-read the sentence — trim it later.
